## The documentary problem

REPLACE: *How William Shatner Changed the World* (2005) is the source for both the Cooper and the Perlman claims.[^shatner] What does it mean when a celebratory TV special becomes the evidence?

## The hypospray came first

REPLACE: a real jet injector was already sold as "Hypospray" by 1951.[^ajhp] The show took its name from real medicine. Why do we tell the story the other way round?

## Named after

REPLACE: two links with documents behind them.

- **Space Shuttle Enterprise, 1976.** Planned as *Constitution*; renamed after a letter campaign by fans. White House memos cite "hundreds of thousands of letters".[^io9] President Ford said he was "partial to the name" for his own navy reasons.
- **Qualcomm Tricorder XPRIZE, 2017.** A competition named after the tricorder, won by Final Frontier Medical Devices.[^geekwire]

REPLACE: is naming something after a show the same as being inspired by it?

## What got left behind

REPLACE: the communicator belonged to a future of cooperation and exploration. What arrived were consumer products and a space shuttle that never flew in space. (Enterprise was a test vehicle and never reached orbit.)
