"Ender's Game is a 1985 military science fiction novel by American author Orson Scott Card. Set at an unspecified date in Earth's future, the novel presents an imperiled humankind after two conflicts with an insectoid alien species they dub the buggers. In preparation for an anticipated third invasion, Earth's international military force recruits young children, including the novel's protagonist, Andrew Ender Wiggin, to be trained as elite officers. The children learn military strategy and leadership by playing increasingly difficult war games, including some in zero gravity, where Ender's tactical genius is revealed."[^wiki-enders-game]

Once the commanders start cheering, Ender senses something is wrong. Soon enough, Mazer tells him the truth: All the simulated battles were actually real. Without knowing it, Ender has helped eliminate the bugger race. The military commanders say Ender had to believe it was a game or else the loss of life would have swayed his decisions.

## XTEND
"XTEND AI Robotics Inc is an Israeli defense company that makes human-guided autonomous machine systems. The company was founded as XTEND in Tel Aviv, Israel, and is currently headquartered in Tampa, Florida."[^wiki-xtend] The CEO of XTEND, Aviv Shapira, has stated that his objective is to recreate Ender's Game.[^maccoll]
