## Set in November 2019

REPLACE: the film imagined 2019 from 1982. Tesla used the real 2019 to stage its own future. What is the difference between predicting a date and arriving on it?

## An android of the author

REPLACE: Hanson and Olney's android talked using Dick's own writings.[^dufty] Losing its head on a plane made headlines, with reporters joking it had run away like a replicant.

## The brand curse

REPLACE: the film's skyline is full of real logos: Pan Am, RCA, Koss, Atari. Pan Am went bankrupt nine years later; GE bought and dismantled RCA in 1986; Koss filed for Chapter 11 about two years after the film; Atari's next big release was the E.T. game.[^bnn] Pan Am also flew to the Moon in *2001*. What happens to companies that put themselves into fictional futures?

## What got left behind

REPLACE: the film is about a corporation, Tyrell, that makes people as products. What did Tesla take from it, and what did it leave out?
